Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Mycroft is a personal voice assistant used only by the household that runs it. This policy explains what it does with data, including data from Google Nest through Google's Smart Device Management API.

Who uses it

Only the owner's household. Mycroft has no public users, no accounts and no website sign-up.

Google Nest data

  • With the owner's permission, Mycroft reads the household's Nest thermostat: indoor temperature, humidity, heating mode, target temperature and whether the heating is running.
  • It changes thermostat settings only when the owner asks it to.
  • The access key Google provides is stored only on the owner's own device at home.
  • Nest data is used only to answer the owner's questions and carry out their requests. It isn't stored long term, sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Voice and replies

  • Speech is converted to text on the device itself. Recordings aren't kept.
  • To work out a reply, the text of a request, plus any relevant information such as the thermostat reading, is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. It is used only to generate that reply.

Removing access

The owner can remove Mycroft's access to Nest at any time from their Google Account, under Third-party apps and services, or from the Nest permissions in the Google Home app.

Use of Google data

Mycroft's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
