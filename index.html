Mycroft

Mycroft is a personal voice assistant that runs on a Raspberry Pi in a private home in London. It is named after Mycroft Holmes, Sherlock's older brother, who rarely left his armchair yet knew everything happening in the city.

It answers spoken questions, shows local travel times and news on a small screen, and, with the owner's permission, reads and adjusts the home's own Nest thermostat.

Mycroft is a private, single-household project. It isn't offered to the public and has no other users.
